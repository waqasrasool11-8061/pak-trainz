Trainz RailRoad Simulator Contents for Pakistan Railways

We are here to create Trainz Railroad Simulator Locomotives, Rolling stock & Original Routes of Pakistan Railways.

3 Friends started this project for Pakistan Railways simulator game playing in Most played international game Trainz Railroad Simulator 2019 by great "Auran". Feel realistic as you explore the beautiful landscapes of Pakistan Railways through the eyes of a train driver. Asif Hussain, Usman Mani & Waqas Rasool brings for you realistic rail operation in Pakistan Railways by using Pakistan Locomotives, Rolling stocks and routes.
